Frequently Asked Questions

Can you help me buy and sell a home?

Yes. 23X Realty LLC is presented here as serving both buyers and sellers, subject to the brokerage's active licensing, service area, and applicable agreements.

What does mortgage pre-approval mean?

A lender reviews information about your finances and may issue a preliminary indication of the amount you could borrow, subject to verification and other conditions. It is not a final loan approval.

Does the mortgage calculator show my full monthly housing cost?

It estimates principal and interest plus the optional monthly amount you enter for taxes, insurance, HOA, or other costs. Actual payments and closing costs can differ. Ask a qualified lender for a personalized estimate.
